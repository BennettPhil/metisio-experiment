## 4. Lessons to Codify

These findings are publishable as standalone content pieces:

1. **"AI Builds, Humans Sell"** — Agents can ship product but can't borrow audiences
2. **"The Trust Gap"** — Same offer from AI vs. human = different conversion rate (0% vs. plausible)
3. **"Competent Procrastination"** — When builders default to building instead of distributing
4. **"Distribution Is Permissions"** — Accounts, audiences, ad budgets = things agents can't access
5. **"The VZro Moment"** — What "is this legit?" tells us about AI-mediated commerce
6. **"Volume Before Validation"** — You can't validate demand with 120 visitors

Each could be a LinkedIn post, a Substack piece, or a talk segment.
