Satya Nadella (Microsoft CEO) dropped a line that should make every SaaS founder slightly uncomfortable:

> **“The traditional application layer is collapsing into agents.”**

I don’t care whether you think “agents” are hype.

I care about the *mechanical consequence* of that statement.

Because if it’s even 30% true, a lot of SaaS products are about to become invisible.

---

## The practical translation

If Nadella is right, then:

- Your **UI** stops being “the product.”
- Your UI becomes **one** interface.
- The **default interface** becomes: *an agent calling your product programmatically.*

The winners are the products that can be:

- discovered,
- authenticated against,
- called safely,
- and debugged,

…without a human clicking through your navigation like it’s 2012.

---

## The 6 things that actually matter

I built a free scorecard that checks the basics in 2 minutes:

**→ https://www.botlington.com/score**

It’s six yes/no questions:

1) **API exists** (and is documented)
2) **Programmatic auth** (no human browser click required)
3) **Structured data** (JSON, not HTML)
4) **MCP / agent-native interface** (or function-calling SDK)
5) **Scoped permissions** (safe tokens, not god-mode keys)
6) **Observability** (can you see agent activity?)

Score yourself.

If you’re below **4/6**, you’re not “behind.” You’re just not *addressable*.

And products that can’t be addressed get skipped.

---

## What to fix first (if you scored low)

The order matters.

1) **Ship the API** (or at least a minimal read-only one)
2) **Fix auth for software** (service accounts / scoped API keys)
3) **Return structured data** (stop trapping everything in HTML)

Then you can worry about MCP and the “nice” layer.
